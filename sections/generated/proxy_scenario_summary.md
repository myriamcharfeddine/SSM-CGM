# Proxy Scenario Summary

Proxy scenario effects are not causal unless the factual-future channel diagnostic, support checks, and natural-experiment validation pass.

Supported proxy scenarios are `meal_proxy`, `mild_activity_proxy`, `high_activity_proxy`, `sleep_rest_proxy`, and `sedentary_stress_proxy`. Unsupported interventions are `insulin_dose`, `insulin_on_board`, `carbohydrate_grams`, and `medication_dose_timing`.

Unsupported scenarios are not interpreted.
